Phoenix: The Clue That Vanished

First look at Phoenix's art before the game is built. Everything here is pixel art made by tools/make_phoenix_sprites.mjs, in the same chunky style as Sojourner.

The scene

The icy northern plain: flat horizon, polygon cracks in the ground, still neon rusty red. Phoenix stays put; its arm reaches out and digs the trench, and the hard icy layer shows at the bottom.

Phoenix and its arm

The lander has no face. The character is the arm, as in Person 1's story ("I was the arm with the scoop"). Its eyes sit on the wrist, next to the Robotic Arm Camera (the dark box on top), which really was mounted on the arm just above the scoop.

Find what vanished

The camera's two pictures of the same trench. Three ice chunks on Sol 20; four sols later two are gone and one has shrunk. The pale pebble is warm white and stays (white alone is not proof of ice). The dump pile grew: that is new, not gone.

Cases 3 and 4 use Blink: one picture that flips between Earlier and Later, the way scientists compare.

EARLIER · Sol 20

Pieces

Real hardware kept

  • Two round, ten-sided solar arrays.
  • Three legs with footpads; the lander never moves.
  • Stereo camera mast (SSI) and the small weather mast.
  • Scoop: blade on the front; the rasp comes out of the back for hard ice.
  • Robotic Arm Camera on the arm, above the scoop.

Style rules followed

  • One dark outline, two tones per part, big eyes.
  • Ice is cool blue-white; the pebble is warm white, so they never look alike.
  • The icy layer is pale and speckled, not a blue pool (it must never look like a puddle).
  • Shadows fall down and to the right; one seamless ground.
  • Gas wisps on the shrinking chunk are ILLUSTRATION.

For you to decide

  • Face on the arm's wrist (as drawn), or on the lander's deck instead?
  • Does the scoop read as a scoop? It is drawn from above as an open pan with a bright front blade.
  • Is the scoop the right size next to the lander? It is drawn bigger than real so its face reads.
