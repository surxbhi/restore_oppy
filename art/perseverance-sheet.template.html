The real code

Seen from below, like the camera on the backshell saw it. The inner rings spell DARE, MIGHTY, THINGS. The band holds JPL's address. Each letter is 7 stripes. Orange means "add this number". A is 1, B is 2, up to Z, 26.

In the game, a few letters are blank. The child copies each one's stripes from a stripe key (no maths), then presses OPEN. Best moves rise across the four puzzles: 3, 5, 7, 9.

What is real

  • Chute: white and orange slices (gores), a dome, a thin gap, then a band round the edge.
  • The code: 7 stripes per letter, read from the middle out, clockwise, starting after the black slice (NASA coding activity).
  • Perseverance: power unit at the back, no solar panels; two Mastcam-Z cameras are its eyes; SuperCam's window on top; drill on the arm.
  • Backshell: the camera that photographed the parachute sits on it (PIA24431).
