Sojourner

A chunky, easy-to-read rover in the style of classic platform-game characters: big shapes, bold outline, two tones per part, huge eyes. Side view when it drives left or right, back view when it drives up, face when it drives down. All art is drawn in code at 40 × 40 pixels and shown enlarged.

On the board

The loop drives right, down, left and up. Each turn swaps the view with a small hop, the way a platform-game hero turns.

Every frame

Small frame counts on purpose: most of the life comes from code (bounces, hops, dust, shakes).

Size line-up

Sojourner is tiny next to Curiosity and Oppy, like the real rovers.

Real hardware kept

  • Six wheels on a rocker-bogie (three show from the side).
  • The flat solar panel on top: Sojourner ran on sunlight.
  • An antenna at the back.
  • The rock tool (APXS) on its back: a grey box with a pink sensor head, seen in the side and back views. Sojourner backed up to rocks to touch them with it.
  • A front camera; the eyes sit on the body, never on the wheels or the tool.

Style rules

  • One dark outline around every shape.
  • Two tones per part: a light colour and one shade.
  • No small detail that turns to noise when the sprite is small.
  • Eyes take a third of the body, with a white shine.
  • Every view fills the same 34 × 38 box (same panel, body and ground line), so Sojourner never changes size when it turns.
